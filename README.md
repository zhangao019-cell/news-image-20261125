Sum up 25 November 2026's world news in one image: a one-page newspaper
front page with 24 stories across 7 regions and 13 fields, a UTC timeline,
a world map, and how-sure labels for every story.

## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) decodes the front-page image
stored as base64 in `entry.png.b64.0` and `entry.png.b64.1` and writes it to
`/output/entry.png`. It runs with no network and writes identical bytes on
every run.

## The image

"The World in One Day" — Wednesday 25 November 2026: Shenzhou-24 lifts off
with the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong, the US proposes tariffs on 14
trading partners, and Asian chip stocks rout (KOSPI -6.2%), plus 21 more
stories, a UTC timeline, and a world map.
